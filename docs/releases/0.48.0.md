In **Copy to…** or **Move to…**, choose **Review space and alternatives…**. Read the destination-content allowance, possible replacement recovery copies, reported capacity and known shortfall before choosing to start. Working payload is counted once; preserving an earlier destination may need additional space. When both demands have the same observed storage-volume UUID, the review combines them against the lower free-space observation.
